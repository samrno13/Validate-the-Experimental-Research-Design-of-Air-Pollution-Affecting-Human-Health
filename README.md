**Validate the Experimental Research Design of Air Pollution Affecting Human Health**

**Author:** Samuel Reano

**Format:** R Markdown Notebook (.Rmd + .nb.html)

**Overview**

The data was collected by a researcher who conducted the experiment. We estimated the relationship between human health and distance to the nearest source of air pollution using three different regression models (human health vs distance to the nearest source of air pollution, human weight vs distance to the nearest source of air pollution, number of admissions to the hospital vs distance to the nearest source of air pollution), determined whether those regression models are good predictors for their respective response variables, explained whether there was causality in the experiment, estimated elasticity of human health with respect to distance to the nearest source of air pollution by using one of the regression models and plotting that model.

**Contents**

* Background information of the dataset
* Three Regression Models for estimating the relationship between human health and distance to the nearest source of air pollution
* The Validation of the Experimental Design
* Elasticity of health with respect to the distance to the nearest source of air pollution.
